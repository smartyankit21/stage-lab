Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Stage Lab is a personal stock-analysis website for Indian markets (NSE and BSE). Market pages work without signing in.

What we collect when you sign in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Stage Lab. We use them only to recognise you and show who is signed in.

What we store

  • Your account (email, name, picture) and the watchlists and trade-journal entries you create.
  • This is kept in a Supabase database. Each record is tied to your account, and the database only lets you read or change your own records.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't access your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

Deleting your data

You can remove watchlist and journal entries at any time on the site. To delete your account and everything in it, email info.ankit21@gmail.com.

Not investment advice

Everything on Stage Lab is for information and learning only.
